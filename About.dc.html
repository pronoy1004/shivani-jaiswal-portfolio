Meet Shivani

Hello, I'm Shivani!

Art has been part of my life for as long as I can remember. What started as a love for drawing and painting eventually became the work I wanted to do every day.

I have completed a BFA and MFA in Fine Arts and have over 10 years of experience as an artist. I'm currently pursuing advanced atelier training at Samsara Academy of Art, Hyderabad, which is affiliated with the Barcelona Academy of Art, Spain.

Over the years, I've created everything from studio paintings and custom portraits to pet portraits, spiritual artworks, abstract paintings and live wedding paintings. While the subjects may vary, my purpose remains the same: to create artwork that feels personal and truly meaningful to its owner.

View My Work

What I Love About Painting

I love creating artwork that means something to the person receiving it.

Sometimes I work quietly in my studio from photographs and references. Other times, I am painting live in the middle of a wedding while everything is happening around me.

Live wedding painting has become a very special part of my work. I love watching the celebration unfold, noticing the little details, and seeing guests stop by to watch the painting come to life.

The best part is always seeing the reaction when the finished painting is revealed.

My Approach

Every painting starts with understanding what you have in mind.

Before I begin, we talk about the subject, size, colours, style, and the details that are important to you.

Some paintings are created from photographs, while live wedding paintings are created at the venue as the celebration happens.

No matter what I am painting, my goal is simple — to create something that feels personal, meaningful, and truly yours.
