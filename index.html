Hello, I'm Shivani

I have loved drawing and painting for as long as I can remember.

I hold a BFA and MFA in Fine Arts and have been working as an artist for over 10 years. I am currently pursuing advanced atelier training at Samsara Academy of Art, Hyderabad, which is affiliated with the Barcelona Academy of Art, Spain.

Over the years, I've created everything from studio paintings and custom portraits to pet portraits, spiritual artworks, abstract paintings and live wedding paintings.

While the subjects may vary, my purpose remains the same: to create artwork that feels personal and truly meaningful to its owner.

Live Painting, Capturing the Moment as It Happens

There is something truly special about watching a blank canvas slowly become part of your celebration.

For live weddings, I arrive before the main moment, set up at the venue, and begin building the scene. As the celebration unfolds, the people, colours, and little details come to life on the canvas.

By the end of the celebration, the painting is complete, ready to be revealed and cherished for years to come.

A Few Common Questions

What kind of artwork do you create?

I create live wedding paintings, portraits, pet portraits, religious and spiritual art, abstract pieces, and custom commissioned artwork.

Do you only paint weddings?

No. Live wedding painting is one part of my work. I also take personal commissions for portraits, pets, spiritual artwork, abstract pieces, and other custom ideas.

What medium do you use for live wedding paintings?

I use acrylic paint for live weddings because it allows me to work efficiently at the venue and complete the artwork during the event.

Is the wedding painting completed at the venue?

Yes. The painting is completed at the wedding venue and does not go through a separate studio-finishing stage afterwards.

Can you create a painting from a photograph?

Yes. Many portraits and custom commissions begin with photographs or other visual references.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes. I travel for selected weddings across India and can also discuss destination and international enquiries depending on availability.
