What Is Live Wedding Painting?

Live wedding painting means creating an original painting at your wedding while the celebration is happening.

I paint in acrylic at the venue and usually spend around six to eight hours creating the artwork.

The moment can be your varmala, pheras, vows, first dance, mandap, or another part of the wedding that feels special to you.

Your guests can watch the painting take shape throughout the event.

By the end of the celebration, the painting is complete and ready for the final reveal.

What Happens on Your Wedding Day?

I usually arrive at the venue two to three hours before the main celebration begins.

I set up my painting space and start by painting the background, including the venue, flowers, décor, and other details.

Once the couple arrives, I take reference photographs of different moments during the wedding. I then choose the moments that work best for the painting.

If you would like to choose, I can show you a few options and you can pick your favourite.

Once the moment is selected, I paint the couple and complete the artwork during the event.

Why Choose a Live Wedding Painting?

You already have photographs and videos, so why add a painting? Because a painting feels different. It is created right there, in the middle of your celebration.

Guests can stop by, watch it grow, and come back later to see how much has changed.

It can also become a beautiful surprise. Sometimes the groom books it for the bride. Sometimes the bride surprises the groom. It can also be a gift from parents, siblings, or friends.

One of my favourite parts is seeing the reaction when the finished painting is revealed.

A painting can also include someone important who could not be there. If a loved one has passed away, we can discuss adding them carefully using a photograph.

The final artwork becomes more than a picture of the wedding. It becomes something personal that you can keep in your home for years.

Which Wedding Moment Should You Choose?

There is no single perfect moment. Every wedding is different.

If you are unsure, I can help you choose the moment that will work best on canvas.

During the event, I take several reference photographs and look for moments that feel natural, happy, and full of emotion. Here are some popular choices.

Varmala

This is one of my most requested moments. The exchange of garlands, laughter, movement, and reactions from family and friends create a painting full of energy.

Pheras

The sacred fire, warm light, rituals, and family gathered around the couple make this a beautiful and emotional moment to paint.

The Mandap

Sometimes the setting itself becomes an important part of the painting. If you have spent months planning the flowers, colours, and décor, the mandap can create a beautiful background for the couple.

Wedding Vows

Vows can create a calm and elegant painting. The couple, altar, aisle, and quiet emotion of the ceremony all work beautifully together.

Reception Entry

Music, lights, confetti, and the excitement of the entrance can make this a fun and lively painting.

Sunset Portrait

Golden hour is one of my favourite times to paint. It works especially well for outdoor, beach, palace, and destination weddings.

Family Moments

Sometimes the most meaningful moment is not part of the ceremony. It may be your parents watching, your grandparents smiling, or your whole family together.

Choose Your Canvas Size

I offer three main canvas sizes as a starting point. The final size is agreed once we have talked through your venue and the moment you want painted.

18 × 24inches

A smaller size that keeps the focus mainly on the couple. The background is simpler so the bride and groom remain the main focus.

24 × 30inches

A good option if you want the couple along with more of the mandap, stage, flowers, or décor.

24 × 36inches

A larger canvas for couples who want more of the venue and surroundings included. This size allows space for architecture, greenery, décor, and more background detail.

Common Questions

How far in advance should we book?

It is best to enquire as soon as your wedding date is confirmed. Six to twelve months in advance is usually comfortable, especially during the busy wedding season from October to February. I take a limited number of weddings, so popular dates can fill early.

Our wedding is only a few weeks away. Is it too late?

Not always. If the date is available and travel can be arranged, I may still be able to paint your wedding. It is always worth asking.

How long does a live wedding painting take?

I usually paint for around six to eight hours at the venue. I arrive before the main moment so I can begin the background in advance.

Is the painting completed at the wedding?

Yes. The painting is completed during the wedding and is ready for the final reveal before the event ends.

What happens if the wedding runs late?

That is completely fine. Wedding timings often change. I plan around the actual celebration, not only the printed schedule.

Will you disturb the photographer or videographer?

No. I choose my painting spot carefully and can coordinate with your planner and photography team beforehand.

Do we need to do anything during the wedding?

No. Enjoy your wedding. I will take care of the painting.

Can guests watch you paint?

Yes. That is one of the nicest parts of live wedding painting. Guests often stop by throughout the event to see how the painting is coming along.

What paint do you use?

I use high quality acrylic paint for live weddings. Acrylic works well for live painting because it allows me to work efficiently at the venue and create a finished artwork during the event.

Can family members be added?

Yes. We can discuss important family members before the wedding.

Can pets be included?

Yes. If your pet is attending the wedding, I can include them naturally. If not, I can use a clear photograph.

Can you leave unwanted things out of the painting?

Yes. The painting does not have to include every detail that appears in a photograph. Things like signs, chairs, equipment, or other distractions can be left out.

Do we receive the original painting?

Yes. You receive the original hand painted artwork.

Do we get a process video?

Yes. A process reel can be created so you can see the painting come to life.

Can you paint our wedding if it has already happened?

Yes. If your wedding has already taken place, I can create a wedding portrait using your photographs. This would be created as a studio painting rather than a live wedding painting.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes. I travel for selected weddings across India and can also discuss international weddings. Travel and stay details are shared based on the location.

Do you work with wedding planners?

Yes. I am happy to coordinate with your wedding planner before the event so the setup and timing are smooth.
